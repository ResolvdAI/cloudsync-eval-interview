## Plan Changes

### Upgrading Your Plan
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Change Plan"
3. Select new plan
4. Confirm payment
5. Immediate access to new features

### Downgrading Your Plan
1. Ensure under storage limit of new plan
2. Go to Settings > Billing
3. Click "Change Plan"
4. Select lower plan
5. Changes apply next billing cycle

### What Happens When You Exceed Limits?

#### Storage Limit Exceeded
- Can't upload new files
- Can still access and download
- 30-day grace period to reduce usage
- Files not deleted automatically

#### User Limit Exceeded
- Can't add new users
- Existing users maintain access
- Upgrade required to add more

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes, you can switch at any time. When switching to annual, you'll be charged the prorated amount for the remainder of the year.

### What happens to my files if I cancel?
Your files remain accessible for 30 days after cancellation. You can download them but cannot upload new files. After 30 days, files may be deleted.

### Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees for any plan. Enterprise customers may incur professional services fees for custom deployments.

### Can I get a custom plan?
Yes, for organizations with 50+ users or special requirements. Contact sales@cloudsync.com.

### Do prices include taxes?
Prices shown exclude applicable taxes. Sales tax, VAT, or GST will be added based on your location.

### How do I get a receipt/invoice?
- Receipts sent automatically via email
- Download from Settings > Billing > Invoices
- Enterprise: Custom invoicing available

### Can I pause my subscription?
Not directly, but you can downgrade to Free plan and upgrade later. Your files and settings are preserved.
